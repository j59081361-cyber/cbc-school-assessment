CBC school results analytics

Paste Grade 1 to 9 marks to see achievement levels, learning area and stream analysis, KPSEA and KJSEA projections, report forms and parent messages.

Save each assessment to compare progress later. Learners are matched by name.

Columns: Name, Stream, then one column per learning area (English, Kiswahili, Mathematics, Integrated Science, Social Studies, Pre-Technical Studies, Agriculture and so on). Leave a mark blank if the learner did not sit it. For the Grade 6 KPSEA projection add an SBA column (Grades 4 to 6 average). For the Grade 9 KJSEA projection add SBA (Grades 7 and 8 average) and KPSEA (Grade 6 score). All out of 100.
